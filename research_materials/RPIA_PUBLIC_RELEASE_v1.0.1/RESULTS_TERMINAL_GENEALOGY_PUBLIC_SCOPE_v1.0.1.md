- Exact shared-Lorentz covariance forces equal limiting coefficients conditionally, but does not explain why different sectors share that covariance.
- Orbit classification fixes the **shape** of an admitted component, not the existence, stability, species assignment or invariant level of that component.
- A worldline parameter equals infrared inertial mass only inside the restricted pointlike, spinless, first-order, exact-reparametrization, full-Lorentz, metric-ultralocal class after the declared normalization.
- Canonical and kinetic momentum coincide only when gauge coupling is absent or appropriately trivialized.
- Pole, running, bare, gravitational, composite, resonance and infrared inertial masses are not identified without additional theory and calibration.
- The decimal \(299\,792\,458\,\mathrm{m\,s^{-1}}\) is fixed by the SI definition and is not a unit-independent dynamical target.
- The frozen RG, spin-2, conformal-normalization, photon-operational and cycles branches do not establish a universal origin mechanism.
