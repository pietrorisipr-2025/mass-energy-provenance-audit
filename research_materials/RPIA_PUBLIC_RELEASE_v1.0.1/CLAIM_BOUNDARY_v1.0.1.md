3. After quotienting definitions, unit conventions, coordinate choices, representation conventions and previously established theorems, the **three audited genealogies share a five-element empirical residue**:

   - physical identification of measured momentum with the standard linear Lorentz covector representation;

   - empirical identification/calibration of the reported mass to the infrared inertial coefficient.
4. The five-element intersection is **route-set relative**. It proves commonality across the audited global, fiberwise and minimal genealogies; it does not prove that every future genealogy must contain exactly and only those five inputs.
