## Correction 1 — interpretation of the three-route intersection

The v1.0.0 wording could be read as if the exact five-element intersection proved an exhaustive, universally irreducible set of empirical inputs. The correct statement is narrower:

> the five nodes are the **common empirical residue of the audited genealogies**.

A future route may require an additional route-specific input while leaving the common five-element intersection unchanged. Therefore the intersection is evidentially useful but not discriminating proof of empirical independence or universal exhaustiveness.

## Correction 2 — mass node typing

The mass node contains two layers that are now written separately:

- `m_in` as the coefficient defined by `E = E0 + |p|^2/(2 m_in) + O(p^4)` — `DEFINITION / OPERATIONAL CONVENTION`;
- identification/calibration of the reported mass with that coefficient — `EMPIRICAL_INPUT`.

The empirical residue remains five nodes; the definitional layer is not counted as a sixth empirical input.

## Correction 3 — translations/Noether are not a sixth common residue

Global translation invariance and Noether conservation are required in the global-Poincare construction of global conserved charges, but they are not prerequisites of the fiberwise/local-shell theorem. That route explicitly works without global translations or globally conserved energy. Therefore they cannot be promoted to a sixth input common to all three audited routes.
